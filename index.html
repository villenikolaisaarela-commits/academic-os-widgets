Framewise widgets

Embeddable widgets for Framewise Academy, a student planner template for Notion.

  • Clock: analog clock with digital time and date.
  • Focus timer: study, short break and long break.
  • Whiteboard: several boards, pens, paper styles and PNG export.
  • Clock + timer: both in one compact widget.
  • Study playlist: a wrapper for Spotify's player.

The clock, timer and whiteboard make no network requests and use no cookies or tracking. What you draw or time stays in your own browser. The study playlist loads Spotify's player, which follows Spotify's own privacy policy.
