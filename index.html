Framewise widgets

Embeddable widgets for Framewise Academic (a student system for Notion). No tracking, no cookies and no external requests.

  • Clock: analog clock with digital time and date. Embed URL: …/clock.html
  • Focus timer: Pomodoro, short break and long break. Embed URL: …/timer.html
  • Clock + timer: both in one compact widget

In Notion, type /embed, paste the link and resize the block.
